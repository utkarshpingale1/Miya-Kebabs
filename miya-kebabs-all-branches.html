Our Story

A Small Dream.
A Big Journey.


In 2011, Junaid started with a modest 200 sq.ft. restaurant tucked in a parking lot in Nashik. No frills. Just fire, spice, and an uncompromising commitment to flavour. That tiny kitchen became the seed of something far bigger.

In 2021, under the parent brand Al Arabian Express, Miya Kebabs was born — built to carry rich Arabian and Mughlai flavours to city after city. Today, the brand stands across Mumbai, Pune, and Nashik with every outlet honouring the same soul-first promise.
