The Miya
Story

Founded with a passion for authentic Mughlai street food, Miya Kebabs started as a humble stall and grew into one of Koregaon Park's most loved eateries. Our roots trace back to the bustling kebab lanes of Mumbai where our founder learned the craft from seasoned ustads.

Every spice blend is hand-crafted. Every marinade is prepared fresh each morning. The tandoor never sleeps — it's the heart of our kitchen, breathing life into everything we make.

We serve more than food. We serve memories — of evening rides to the kebab stall, of smoke and spice in the air, of flavors that stay with you long after the last bite.
