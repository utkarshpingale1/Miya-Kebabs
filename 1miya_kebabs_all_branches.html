Our Journey Since 2011

From a 200 sq.ft
Dream to 8 Cities

What started as a small dream in 2011 became a story of passion, persistence, and people. In the heart of Nashik, inside a modest 200 sq. ft. restaurant, Junaid started a simple vision to serve authentic flavors people would keep coming back for.

In 2021, under the parent brand Al Arabian Express, Miya Kebabs was born — expanding from Nashik to Bandra's Pali Hill, Kalyan, Andheri, Fort, Koregaon Park, and Baner.

2011Founded in Nashik as Al Arabian Express
2021Miya Kebabs brand launched
2022Bandra, Andheri & Fort opened in Mumbai
2023Koregaon Park flagship opened in Pune
2024Viman Nagar & Kalyan branches launched
2025Baner opens — 8 locations and growing!
